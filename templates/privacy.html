Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

CampusPrinter ("we", "us", or "our") is a campus printing system designed to allow authorized users to submit and manage print jobs for supported college laboratory printers.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how CampusPrinter is used, we may collect:

  • Name and email address associated with your account.
  • Authentication and session information.
  • Selected printer and laboratory information.
  • Print job information such as job status, submission time, and printer.
  • Temporary information required to process a print job.

2. Uploaded Documents

Documents submitted through CampusPrinter are processed for the purpose of completing the requested print job.

Documents may be temporarily stored while a print job is pending or being processed. Uploaded documents are not intended to be retained permanently by CampusPrinter.

Documents are transferred using encrypted communication and may also be encrypted during transfer between the CampusPrinter backend and the printer agent.

3. How We Use Information

Collected information may be used to:

  • Authenticate users.
  • Process and manage print jobs.
  • Route print jobs to the selected printer.
  • Send verification emails or OTPs.
  • Monitor printer availability and system health.
  • Maintain and improve the CampusPrinter service.
  • Investigate technical problems and security issues.

4. OTP Verification

CampusPrinter may use a one-time verification code to authorize the release of a print job.

Users should keep verification codes confidential and should only enter them when they are physically present at the selected laboratory and ready to release their print job.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures designed to protect information processed by CampusPrinter, including encrypted network communication, authentication mechanisms, access controls, and encrypted document transfer where applicable.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid submitting confidential or highly sensitive documents unless such use has been approved by the relevant institution.

6. Third-Party Services

CampusPrinter may use third-party services for functions such as authentication, email delivery, hosting, database services, and secure network connectivity.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for authentication, print-job processing, system operation, security, troubleshooting, and legitimate administrative purposes.

Temporary print files may be removed after the corresponding print job has been completed or cancelled.

8. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

  • Keeping their account credentials and OTPs confidential.
  • Submitting only documents they are authorized to print.
  • Checking the selected printer before submitting a print job.
  • Not attempting to interfere with CampusPrinter or its printer agents.

9. Children's and Student Data

CampusPrinter is currently intended for controlled institutional testing with faculty members and authorized laboratory staff. Any future student access may be subject to additional institutional requirements and policies.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as CampusPrinter develops or its functionality changes. Updated versions will be published through the CampusPrinter service.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or concerns, please contact the CampusPrinter project administrators through the official institutional or project contact channel.
